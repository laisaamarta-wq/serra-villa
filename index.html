01The villa

A house laid into the hillside

Two long levels of travertine follow the slope instead of levelling it, so the ridge keeps the shape it had before the house arrived.

The lower level holds the living room, the kitchen, two guest suites and a 28-metre infinity pool that ends at the edge of the cliff. The upper level, entered from the olive garden, holds the master suite, a study and a second suite under a slatted oak pergola. Every room faces the bay.

02Architecture

The view was designed first. The rooms were arranged behind it.

  1. 01

    Follow the slope

    Two long levels step down the hillside instead of levelling it. The slope keeps the shape it had, with pines, limestone and wild rosemary right up to the walls.

    Shown in the drawing: the two levels and the ground line
  2. 02

    Face the bay

    Serra was drawn for one ridge above the sea. Glass walls open every room toward the water, and the 28-metre pool runs out to the cliff edge.

    Shown in the drawing: the glass, the terraces and the pool
  3. 03

    One line of roof

    A single thin roof slab carries the line of the ridge out toward the sea, with a slatted oak pergola over the upper terrace.

    Shown in the drawing: the roof slab and the pergola
